## Feature Proposal: Rule-to-Strategy Wizard (summary)

- Step 1: Enter trading rule (natural language or form input).
- Step 2: Preview parsed rule and tune parameters.
- Step 3: Sandbox backtest with plain-language results.
- Step 4: Export templates (JS/Python) or save strategy.
- Step 5: Choose automation mode (Manual → Assisted → Auto).

Benefits: lower barrier to entry, trust-building, safe experimentation, and a path to scalable automation.

---

## Key Insights

1. Market gap: strong need for an approachable onboarding path.
2. Education and explainability increase adoption.
3. Progressive automation reduces risk and increases trust.
