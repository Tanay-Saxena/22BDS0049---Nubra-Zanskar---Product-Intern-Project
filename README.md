## Deliverables

### 1. Competitor Analysis

A structured review of backtesting and algo-trading platforms in the Indian market:

* Strengths and weaknesses of Zerodha Streak, Tradetron, AlgoTest, QuantMan, Quantiply
* Industry gaps: onboarding, explainability, education
* Positioning for Nubra as the **“first bridge to automation”**

---

### 2. Feature Proposal: Rule-to-Strategy Wizard

A guided wizard to help manual traders gradually adopt algo trading:

* **Step 1:** Enter trading rule (natural language or form)
* **Step 2:** Preview and adjust parameters
* **Step 3:** Run sandbox backtest with plain-language results
* **Step 4:** Export templates (JS/Python)
* **Step 5:** Choose automation mode (Manual → Assisted → Auto)

**Benefits:** Low barrier, trust-building, safe experimentation, scalable adoption.

---

### 3. UI/UX Analysis & Improvements

* Current Nubra dashboard: cluttered, jargon-heavy, lacking onboarding.
* Recommendations:

  * Add **Start Wizard** CTA prominently
  * Show **plain-language backtest summaries**
  * Embed **tooltips and micro-lessons** for education
  * Introduce **progress tracker** for onboarding
  * Redesign dashboard with modular cards and simplified hierarchy

## Key Insights

1. **Market Gap:** Competitors focus on advanced users; beginners lack an approachable on-ramp.
2. **Trust & Education:** Clear summaries and contextual help are critical for adoption.
3. **Progressive Automation:** Manual → Assisted → Auto builds user confidence gradually.
4. **Differentiation:** Nubra can win by being the **education-first automation platform**.
5. **Scalable Feature:** The Wizard starts as a sandbox tool but can grow into execution and marketplace integrations.

---

## Implementation Phases

1. **Phase 1:** Build Wizard MVP (input, parse, sandbox backtest).
2. **Phase 2:** Add parameter tuning + export functionality.
3. **Phase 3:** Introduce progressive automation modes.
4. **Phase 4:** Enhance with broker APIs, strategy marketplace, and advanced analytics.

## Conclusion

The **Rule-to-Strategy Wizard** and associated UI/UX redesign directly address Nubra’s opportunity: helping retail traders take their **first confident step into algo trading**.

By focusing on **onboarding, explainability, and progressive automation**, Nubra can differentiate itself from established players like Zerodha and Upstox, positioning itself as the **education-first automation gateway** for the retail trading market.
