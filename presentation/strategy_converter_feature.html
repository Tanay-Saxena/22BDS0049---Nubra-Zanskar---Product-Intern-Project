Nubra — Strategy Converter (Rule→Strategy Wizard)

A guided flow to transform manual rules into backtestable strategy templates — designed for placement demo and in-app evaluation.

Assignment-friendly
Email sign-in only — no Demat needed
Problem
Many retail traders have repeatable, profitable manual rules but lack a safe, easy path to automate them. They need: simple parsing, transparent simulation, and a gradual automation path.

Plain-Language Parser

Enter your rule in natural language. The system highlights parsed components and confidence.
Input: "Buy when 20MA crosses 50MA and RSI > 60"

Guided Parameter Tuning

Adjust timeframe, lookback windows, risk per trade, and sample symbol with helpful defaults.
Defaults: timeframe=15m, risk=1.5% per trade

In-browser Sandbox Backtest

Quick simulations run locally on sample CSV slices — includes win rate, average return, and drawdown.
Result: win_rate=68% | avg_return=1.7% | max_dd=-6.2%

Progressive Automation Modes

Manual → Assisted (signals for approval) → Auto — users choose confidence path.
1 • Observe
System watches your trades (optional) or accept simple rule text.
2 • Parse & Preview
See parsed blocks and edit before simulation.
3 • Simulate
Run a sandbox test and view plain-language metrics.
4 • Automate Gradually
Select Assisted mode to approve signals before full automation.
