# Revenue Projections & Monetization — AlgoBridge

## Monetization philosophy

For conversion-focused features like the R2S Wizard, the initial monetization goal is to *reduce friction and grow paid upgrades*, not immediately monetize every action. Revenue should follow demonstrated value: once users see positive signal in sandbox simulations, conversion to paid features becomes plausible.

## Revenue streams (MVP → Scale)

1. **Feature Upsell (Primary)**
   - Free: Basic R2S Wizard simulations with limited runs and sample datasets.
   - Paid: Premium simulations, longer historical windows, priority templates, and exportable strategy code.

2. **Education & Micro-courses (Secondary)**
   - Short paid modules teaching reliable automation practices, risk controls.

3. **Broker/Partner Integrations (Later-stage B2B)**
   - White-label wizard / licensing to brokers or trading edu platforms.

4. **Execution & Data Add-ons (Scale)**
   - Paper execution fees for simulated broker connections; premium real-time data feeds.

## Pricing snapshots (suggested)

- **Starter** — Free
  - 3 simulations per month, limited data window, access to 5 rule templates.

- **Pro** — ₹799/month or ₹7,999/year
  - Unlimited basic simulations, extended data windows, downloadable strategy skeletons.

- **Growth (Team / Power Trader)** — ₹3,499/month
  - Advanced analytics, batch simulate, CSV export, early beta features.

- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing
  - Licensing, integration, priority support.

## Conservative 12-month projection (assumptions)
- Pilot users (months 4–6): 1,000 active (free)
- Post-pilot paid conversion: 3% of pilot convert to Pro within 90 days
- Organic acquisition & in-app activation months 7–12: 25,000 additional free signups
- Overall converted users by month 12: 1,000 (pilot converts) + 750 (organic converts) = 1,750 paid users
- Average ARPU (paid) = ₹600/month (blended annualized)

**Year 1 revenue (conservative):**
1,750 paid users × ₹600 × 12 = **₹12.6 lakhs** (~₹1.26M)

## Mid-range 24-month projection (scale scenario)
- Reach 50,000 active signups by month 18 via in-app promotion + content.
- Paid conversion (blended): 6% → 3,000 paid users.
- ARPU uplift due to upsells & courses: ₹850/month blended.

**Year 2 revenue (scale):**
3,000 × ₹850 × 12 = **₹30.6 lakhs** (~₹3.06M)

## Unit economics & CAC assumptions
- Initial CAC (pilot phase): ₹200 (email invites, in-app promotion).
- Scale CAC (paid channels): ₹650 (target to optimize down with virality & referral).
- Payback period target: ≤ 6 months for Pro plan subscribers.

## Key levers to improve revenue
1. **Improve conversion** from simulation → export → subscribe by enhancing perceived value (clear metric improvements).
2. **Add paid templates and micro-courses** that directly tie to increased simulated performance.
3. **Introduce light execution fees** when paper-execution / broker sandbox is added.
4. **B2B licensing** to brokers for white-label integration (higher margin, less volume).

## Sensitivity analysis (high level)
- If conversion doubles (from 3% to 6%), Year 1 revenue scales proportionally.
- If ARPU increases by ₹200/month due to premium features, Year 2 revenue improves meaningfully.
- Major upside: a white-label licensing deal with a mid-sized broker would immediately change the trajectory and could replace user-paid revenue as the primary income source.

## Conclusion
AlgoBridge’s early monetization should be conservative and focused on proving value and improving conversion. The MVP economics are modest but scalable: the product is primarily an activation play for broader broker monetization (paper-to-live funnel, later data/execution fees, and B2B licensing).
